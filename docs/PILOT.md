# Evidence and bounded pilot / 证据与有限试点

Specific user: release/support engineers handing off a local import failure with setup prerequisites and several independent error paths. Buyer hypothesis: an engineering lead spending time triaging irreproducible or wrong-error support reproductions. The documented Picire custom tester workflow and Hypothesis shrinking establish the existing job; they do not establish customer demand for this package.

`tools/contrast.py` executes the identical initial manifest, local SQLite fixture, repeat count and call ceiling for four methods: plain signature-blind ddmin (accepts any failure including invalid setup), dependency-closed signature-blind ddmin, dependency-closed signature-aware ddmin and FailureSlice exact. These are small sequential subset/complement implementations with per-run caching, not benchmarked incumbent Picire. Closure adds prerequisites for baseline kept subsets, whereas FailureSlice's exact universe contains only closed subsets and local deletions remove dependents; report final size after closure, and actual invocations rather than nominal queries. Every transcript is retained. Cached final classifications do not add imaginary calls.

Fixture distinctions: an actual SQLite duplicate insertion gives the target signature; malformed legacy JSON gives a different signature; missing setup gives INVALID. The standard-success case disables the legacy path so a well-configured standard reducer can work. The nonmonotone case has a target at the full four steps and at `schema,seed`, but all single dependent-deletions pass. The adverse-budget case adds six irrelevant steps and limits calls to 60: exact enumeration may be UNKNOWN even while a heuristic finds a useful reproducer. This is a falsifiable limitation of exponential certification, not an excuse to call it optimal.

中文试点：给一个 4–12 步且可快速复位的本地失败清单，工程师声明目标签名、前置条件和环境身份。先运行 local 获取小候选，再用足够预算运行 exact，交接真实观测与证书。观察“仍是目标失败”比例、无效调用次数、候选步骤数、实际调用数和人工排查时间；无收益即终止试点。失败签名的设计错误、隐藏状态或昂贵 oracle 都可能抵消收益。

Economic hypothesis only: if a support handoff normally involves 20 minutes of manual deletion/debugging and the fixture oracle takes 1 second, 100 calls cost ~100 seconds of machine time plus manifest authoring/reset cost. Savings require avoided engineer time to exceed that cost; no measured customer savings, pricing, revenue, willingness to pay or adoption is known. Initial bounded pilot: five sanitized local reproductions, <=12 steps, <=512 calls each, two repeats, manually review every reduced reproducer before sharing. Record baseline-success and adverse cases; do not cherry-pick the legacy-error example.

Sources checked 2026-10-03: [Picire official README](https://github.com/renatahodovan/picire/blob/master/README.rst) documents configurable subset/complement search, custom interestingness testers and 1-minimality; [Hypothesis author explanation](https://hypothesis.works/articles/how-hypothesis-works/) explains invariant-preserving shrinking; [current settings tutorial](https://hypothesis.readthedocs.io/en/latest/tutorial/settings.html) documents control of replay/generation phases. A custom Picire predicate could implement the same failure identity contract; differentiation here is a concrete reusable manifest/protocol/observation/certificate combination, not a claim that established tools cannot do it. Novelty or a passing independent score is unknown until review.
